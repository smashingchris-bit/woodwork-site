Woodwork – Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email support.woodworkcc@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Restoring purchases

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Coins cannot be restored; "Remove ads" and kits can.

How to play

Swipe towards the goal to shoot. Curve your swipe for spin. In Crossbar, only hitting the bar counts – every hit moves you further back.
