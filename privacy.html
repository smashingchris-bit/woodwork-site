Woodwork – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Woodwork ("the app") is a football game. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, player name, chosen country, coins and purchases are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device, except what is described under Leaderboards. Deleting the app deletes the local data.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA), IP address, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for permission before your device's advertising identifier is used for tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change these choices at any time in your device settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Usage statistics and crash reports

To improve the game, the app sends anonymous usage events (for example app opened, round finished, mission completed) and error reports to our database provider Supabase, linked to your random account ID. They are not used for advertising and are deleted together with your account.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive a private identifier from Apple (and your email only if you choose to share it) and store a copy of your game progress so you can restore it on another device. You can delete it in Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device only. No data is sent to us.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment details.

Leaderboards

To show online leaderboards, the app creates an anonymous account (no email or password). We store your player name, chosen country, a random account ID and your ranked scores with the dates they were set. Your name, country and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards. This data is stored with our database provider Supabase. You can delete your account and all of this data at any time in Settings → Delete my account and online data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
